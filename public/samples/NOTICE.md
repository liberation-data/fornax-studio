# Inspect samples

The sample images the Inspect window offers.

| File | What it is | Source and license |
|---|---|---|
| `invoice.png` | A supplier's invoice. The supplier, customer, address, items and figures are all made up. | Drawn by `scripts/DrawSamples.java`. Apache-2.0, as the rest of this repository. |
| `tray.png` | A tray of 24 identical machined parts, one with a dent in its rim (row 2, column 4). | Drawn by `scripts/DrawSamples.java`. Apache-2.0. |
| `chest-xray.jpg` | A posteroanterior chest X-ray. | [File:Chest Xray PA 3-8-2010.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chest_Xray_PA_3-8-2010.png) on Wikimedia Commons, by Stillwaterising, released under **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero, public domain dedication). Changed here: the technologist's initials beside the side marker are blanked, and the image is scaled to 1280 px wide, made greyscale and saved as JPEG. |

The X-ray is for trying the window: what a model says about it is an aid for a clinician's review, not a diagnosis.
